Start Hiking in Iowa City

Never hiked before? Good. This guide was written for you. We cover the trails around Iowa City that are friendly to beginners, and we explain the small practical things that most trail listings leave out: where to park, what the ground is like after rain, and what you actually need to carry.

Who this guide is for

  • College students who have no boots, no backpack, and a free Saturday afternoon.
  • Families with young kids who want a walk that is short, safe, and interesting enough to keep little legs moving.
  • Casual walkers who are ready for something more scenic than the neighborhood loop but do not want a steep climb.

What we mean by "beginner-friendly"

When we recommend a trail, it meets most of these tests: you can finish it in under two hours at a relaxed pace, the path is wide and easy to follow, the hills are gentle or optional, and there is a clear place to park and turn around. If a trail gets muddy, steep, or confusing in certain conditions, we say so plainly.

Meet the team

Maya Brandt started First Steps Trail Guide after taking her roommates on their first hike and realizing how many small questions they had. Devon Okafor walks every trail we write about in all four seasons and keeps our notes on mud, ice, and crowds up to date. Priya Lindqvist reviews each page with a simple question in mind: could someone with no experience follow this?

Before you head out

  • Check the weather and sunset time. Lose the light and an easy trail gets harder.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and when you expect to be back.
  • Carry water, even on a short walk.
  • Trail conditions change. Check with the park or trail manager for current closures before you go.
